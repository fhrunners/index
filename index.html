Come run with us.

Forest Hills Runners is a neighborhood running group based in Jamaica Plain. Since 2011, we’ve been bringing people together for easy miles, workouts, long runs, and post-run hangs.

Run at your pace All speeds and experience levels welcome.
Explore the neighborhood Discover JP, Franklin Park, and beyond.
Come for the miles Stay for the people.

Join us for a run!

It's easy to join FHR! No need for membership fees or sign-ups. Just come to the run at the scheduled time, introduce yourself, and start running with us.

Monday Social / Recovery Run

6:30PM at the Forest Hills Bike Rotary

Join us for a relaxed social / recovery run every Monday evening through the SW Corridor and around Jamaica Pond!

Tuesday Morning Run

6:00AM at the Jamaica Pond Boathouse

Start your Tuesday morning with an easy, conversational 4 to 5 mile run along the Emerald Necklace!

Thursday Track Night

7PM at the Downes Field Track (Winter: 7:30PM at the Reggie Lewis Center)

Improve your speed and technique with our Thursday track nights!

The Saturday Run

8:30AM at the Forest Hills Bike Rotary

Join us for a run every Saturday morning, followed by coffee (and ice cream!) at J.P. Licks!
